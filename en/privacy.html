Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy applies to the mobile and web apps published by LB Management Services: the score keeper (Scorini) and the bonsai care log (Racines Carrées).

1. Who we are

Publisher and data controller: LB Management Services, 1 rue de Haarlem, 59200 Tourcoing, France. Contact: studio@lbmservices.fr.

2. In short

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • Your data stays on your device: games, players, scores, trees, photos and settings are stored on your phone (or in your browser for the web version). We never receive them.
  • No analytics tool is built into the apps.
  • On Android, these apps show an advertising banner from Google AdMob, after your consent choice where the law requires it. You can remove it with a one-time purchase.

3. Data stored on your device

This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data (phone settings). Calculations (loan, yield, taxes) run on the device.

It only leaves your device in two cases:

  • Exports you make yourself (section 4).
  • Android backup, if it is turned on for your phone: Android may copy app data to your Google account. Google manages this copy and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in the phone settings.

4. Racines Carrées: camera, photos, reminders, backups

  • Camera: requested when you need it, to photograph your trees and read their labels. The app reads the QR code on the phone, with a Google library (ML Kit). It does not store or send the scan images. ML Kit sends Google technical information, encrypted, for diagnostics and usage statistics: phone model, Android version, app version, performance metrics and error codes.
  • Gallery: the phone's picker lets you choose your photos; the app only receives the ones you choose.
  • GPS location: each photo is resized and saved again without its original data, including the GPS location. If you turn on "Also keep the original photos", the originals keep this data, including in your backups.
  • Reminders: notifications are scheduled on the phone, with no server. Android 13 and later ask for your permission.
  • Backups and labels: an export only happens when you ask. You choose a folder on the phone, or the app that receives the file (Drive, email…): the file is then subject to that service's rules. It is not encrypted and contains your records, journal and photos. Label printing goes through the phone's print service.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob, Android)

The score keeper and Racines Carrées show a banner provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). To select and display an ad, Google's SDK may process: the device advertising ID, IP address, technical information about the device and app, and your interactions with ads.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google form asks for your choice on first launch. No ad is requested before that choice. If you refuse personalisation, Google may show non-personalised ads, according to its rules.
  • Change your choice: app Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • Google acts under its own responsibility for this processing: how Google uses information from partner sites and apps, Google Privacy Policy.

No ads are shown in the web version.

6. "Remove ads" purchase

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play (or the App Store): we receive neither your details nor your payment method. The app only receives the purchase confirmation, kept on the device, and can request it again from the store ("Restore purchase").

7. Statistics and crashes

We do not track your usage. Google Play provides us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (installs, active users, crashes reported by Android if you allowed it in your phone settings).

8. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Data on the device: it is not sent to us; it serves the features you use.
  • Personalised advertising and access to the advertising ID: your consent, collected by Google's form.
  • Non-personalised ads: your consent, where they require access to information on your device; otherwise, our legitimate interest in funding the free app.
  • Purchase: performance of the sales contract, handled by Google Play.

9. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability. The data in section 3 is under your sole control (edit or delete it in the app, or clear the app's data). For advertising and purchases, Google is your contact; for any question, write to studio@lbmservices.fr. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL or your local supervisory authority.

10. Children

The apps are intended for users aged 13 and over; they are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data about children (and in any case we do not receive the data entered in the apps: see section 3). If you believe a child under 13 has used one of the apps, you can clear its data in the phone settings or write to us at studio@lbmservices.fr.

If you are under 15 and live in France, consent to personalised advertising must be given with a parent or guardian. If in doubt, refuse it in the form. Racines Carrées is intended for users aged 16 and over.

11. Changes

Any significant change to this policy will be shown in the app or on this page, with its update date.
